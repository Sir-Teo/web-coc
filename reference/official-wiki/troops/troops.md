## Mechanics

- Elixir troops are trained in the Barracks (unlocked by Barracks level) and researched with Elixir; Dark Elixir troops come from the Dark Barracks and are researched with Dark Elixir.
- Super Troops are temporary boosts of base troops and are not part of these lists (client rows flagged `EnabledBySuperLicence`).
- Wiki Elixir Troops list (19): Barbarian, Archer, Giant, Goblin, Wall Breaker, Balloon, Wizard, Healer, Dragon, P.E.K.K.A, Baby Dragon, Miner, Electro Dragon, Yeti, Dragon Rider, Electro Titan, Root Rider, Thrower, Meteor Golem.
- Wiki Dark Elixir Troops list (13): Minion, Hog Rider, Valkyrie, Golem, Witch, Lava Hound, Bowler, Ice Golem, Headhunter, Apprentice Warden, Druid, Furnace, Ruin Witch.
