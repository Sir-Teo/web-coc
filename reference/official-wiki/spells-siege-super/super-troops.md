## Mechanics

- Super Troops are temporary upgrades of existing Home Village troops: stronger stats, more housing space and usually a unique ability. They are boosted at the Super Sauna, which appears automatically at Town Hall 11.
- Requirements: Town Hall 11 or higher and the base troop researched to that Super Troop's minimum level (table below; several therefore need TH12, and Super Miner / Super Yeti / Super Hog Rider need TH13).
- Cost and duration: 25,000 Dark Elixir or one Super Potion per boost; the Super version is then trainable in the Barracks / Dark Barracks for 3 days, alongside the normal version. A Playground trial can be played before boosting.
- At most two boosts can be active at once (three during a Super Troop Spotlight, one of which must be the featured troop). If the army/queue already holds the maximum number of different Super Troop types, another type cannot be boosted until all units of one type are removed or used.
- Research mapping: Super Troops have no research of their own; the boosted troop's level equals the researched level of its base troop, and a base-troop upgrade that completes during the boost upgrades the Super Troop immediately.
- When the 3 days end the Super option disappears from the Barracks, but queued Super Troops still finish and trained ones stay in the Army Camps until used. There is no cooldown before boosting again.
- A non-Spotlight boost can be cancelled at any time: no refund, and all units from that boost are removed from camps and queues, freeing the slot for a different boost.
- Donations: Super Troops can be donated like normal troops (even to players who cannot boost them yet) if the receiving Clan Castle's Laboratory cap allows the base troop's minimum boost level; Quick Donate requires the donor to have unlocked the Super Troop.
- Trained Super Troops leave the Barracks looking like the normal troop, walk into the Super Sauna and come out transformed (visual only).
- When a Clan Castle or Troop Launcher releases several troops, Super Troops sort with other troops by housing space and then by internal ID (see clan-castle.md).

## Client comparison

**Which client columns encode each mechanic**

- per-troop boost row -> super_licences: `Original`, `MinOriginalLevel` (0-based), `Replacement`, `DurationH` 72, `CooldownH` 72, `Resource` DarkElixir, `ResourceCost` 25000, `TrialNPC` (Playground trial NPC level)
- global limits -> globals `MAX_ACTIVE_SUPER_LICENCES` = 2, `MIN_TH_LEVEL_FOR_SUPER_LICENCES` = 10 (0-based TH index -> TH11)
- unit rows -> characters rows with `EnabledBySuperLicence` = TRUE; `VisualLevel` = base troop level; production building Barracks/Dark Barracks
- effective TH -> max(TH11, TownHallLevel of the Laboratory level stored in the base troop's `LaboratoryLevel` at the minimum level)

**Notes, ambiguities and manual checks**

- `MinOriginalLevel` and `MIN_TH_LEVEL_FOR_SUPER_LICENCES` are 0-based in the client: every one of the 17 rows is exactly one below the wiki's minimum level, and 10 means TH11.
- Cooldown mismatch: every client licence row has `CooldownH` = 72, but the Super Sauna page states there is no cooldown between boosts. Do not implement a cooldown.
- Super Sauna page lists Super Wall Breaker's requirement as Wall Breaker level 8, while the Super Wall Breaker page and the client say level 7 (Wall Breaker 8 would need Laboratory 10 / TH12, contradicting the Sauna's own lab 9 / TH11 columns).
- The Spotlight 'three boosts' rule has no client global (only `MAX_ACTIVE_SUPER_LICENCES` = 2); it must come from event configuration.
- Cost/duration defaults come from Template:SuperTroopBoost (rev 598512), transcluded by every Super Troop page without overrides, and are restated on the Super Sauna page.
