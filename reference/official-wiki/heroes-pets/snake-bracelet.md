# Snake Bracelet

- **Source:** https://clashofclans.fandom.com/wiki/Snake_Bracelet
- **Wiki revision:** 621017 (retrieved 2026-09-15 via the MediaWiki API)
- **Category:** equipment · **Hero:** Barbarian King

- **Also used:** [Snake Bracelet/Snake](https://clashofclans.fandom.com/wiki/Snake_Bracelet/Snake) (revision 619944)

> King Epic passive item: spawns wall-jumping Snakes for every 300 damage he takes (cap 11 → 54); passive DPS, HP and attack speed.

## Mechanics

- Epic equipment for the Barbarian King; ability type **Passive**; unlock/obtain: Buy in Snake Festival event for 3,100 Snake Medal; Buy in Dark Deal event for 3,100 Mischief Medal; Buy in Equipment Blast event for 3,100 Relic Medal; or purchasable from the Trader for 1,500 Gem.
- Levels 1–27. Blacksmith level required by equipment level: 1–12 → 1; 13–15 → 3; 16–18 → 5; 19–21 → 7; 22–24 → 8; 25–27 → 9.
- Total ore from level 1 to max (sum of wiki costs): 56,060 Shiny Ore, 3,720 Glowy Ore, 480 Starry Ore.
- Per-level stats (level 1 → max): Max Summoned Snakes 11 → 54; Snake Level 1 → 10; Damage per Second Increase 10 → 75; Hitpoint Increase 150 → 4,050; Attack Speed Increase 1% → 10%.
- Passive ability: every 300 damage the King receives summons a Snake; a single hit above 300 can summon several. Total Snakes per battle are capped: 11, 14, 17, 20, 24, 32, 40, 46, 50, 54 by tier.
- Snakes jump over Walls and attack single ground targets (no preferred target); Snake level = item tier (1 at levels 1–2 … 10 at 27): 260 → 500 HP, 32 → 50 DPS, 1-second attacks, wiki speed 24, 1 housing space. Compared with Snake Barrel snakes they have more HP but less DPS (equal at max).
- Passive: +10 → +75 DPS, +150 → +4,050 HP (the largest HP bonus of any equipment), +1% → +10% attack speed.
- Obtain: 3,100 event medals (Snake Festival, Dark Deal, Equipment Blast) or 1,500 gems at the Trader.

**Matches (every wiki level checked against the inherited client rows)**

- `damagePerSecondIncrease` = `character_items.DPS` (all levels)
- `hitpointIncrease` = `character_items.HitPoints` (all levels)
- `attackSpeedIncreasePercent` = `character_items.AttackSpeedPercentage` (all levels)
- `blacksmithLevelRequired` = `character_items.RequiredBlacksmithLevel` (all levels)
- Ore costs: the wiki cost on level N equals client row N−1 `UpgradeResources`/`UpgradeCosts` (CommonOre = Shiny, RareOre = Glowy, EpicOre = Starry) for every level
- `maxSummonedSnakes` = `MainAbilities:special_abilities[BarbarianKingSpawnOnTakeDamage].MaxActivations` at the item's `MainAbilityLevels`/`ExtraAbilityLevels` (same value)
- `snakeLevel` = `MainAbilities:special_abilities[BarbarianKingSpawnOnTakeDamage].SelfSpellLevel` at the item's `MainAbilityLevels`/`ExtraAbilityLevels` (same value)

- Summoned unit Snake Bracelet/Snake: `hitpoints` vs `characters[BK Equipment Snake].Hitpoints`: all levels match
- Summoned unit Snake Bracelet/Snake: `damagePerSecond` vs `characters[BK Equipment Snake].DPS`: all levels match

**Client columns and interpretation notes**

- `special_abilities[BarbarianKingSpawnOnTakeDamage]`: `ActiveAfterTakingDamage` 300, `MaxActivations` = Snake cap, `DeactivateAfterMasterDies=TRUE` (no spawns after the King falls), `SelfSpell=BarbKingEquipSpawn` at the tier.
- `spells[BarbKingEquipSpawn]`: `SummonTroop=BK Equipment Snake`, `UnitsToSpawn` 1, `Radius` 200, `RandomRadius` 150, `SpawnFirstGroupSize` 2, `SpawnUpgradeLevel` = Snake level.
- `characters[BK Equipment Snake]`: `Speed` 300, `AttackRange` 40 (0.4 tiles; wiki shows ???), `AttackSpeed` 1000, ground-only, `IsJumper=TRUE`, `FriendlyGroupWeight` 50.

**Mismatches / ambiguities**

- `spawnFirstGroupSize`: wiki **one Snake per 300 damage** vs client **BarbKingEquipSpawn SpawnFirstGroupSize 2** — client may spawn two Snakes on the first trigger; unverified
