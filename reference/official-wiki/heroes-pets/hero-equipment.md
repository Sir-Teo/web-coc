## Mechanics

- Every hero equips two items on attack (none on defense). Active items change what the hero's once-per-battle ability does; passive items work continuously. All items (except Eternal Tome) also grant passive stat boosts (hitpoints, DPS, attack speed, recovery or self-healing).
- Items are upgraded in the Blacksmith with ore; Common items cap at 18, Epic at 27. Common items are starting items or Blacksmith unlocks; Epic items come from event medal shops (3,100 medals) and later the Trader (1,500 gems); some are also sold in the League Shop (750 medals: Giant Gauntlet, Frozen Arrow).
- Ore pattern (identical for all items of a rarity): Shiny every level (120 at level 2 → 2,700 at Common level 18 / 3,600 at Epic level 27), Glowy on every third level (20, 100, 200, 400, 600…), Starry only on Epic items from level 9 on every third level (10, 20, 30, 50, 100, 120, 150). Totals: Common 27,260 Shiny + 1,920 Glowy; Epic 56,060 Shiny + 3,720 Glowy + 480 Starry.
- Ability attributes change in tiers — for most items at levels 1, 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18 (and 21, 24, 27 for Epic) while passive stats change every level; Invisibility Vial, Eternal Tome, Frozen Arrow, Fireball and Lavaloon Puppet instead have a separate ability row for every level (values may still repeat).
- Equipment Migration (December 2023): starting items were upgraded for free according to hero level, ignoring Blacksmith requirements:
  - King/Queen levels 1–10 → 1, 11–20 → 2, 21–25 → 3, 26–30 → 4, then +1 per five levels to 81–90 → 15;
  - Warden 1–3 → 1, 4–5 → 2, 6–7 → 3, 8–9 → 4, 10–11 → 5, 12–13 → 6, 14–15 → 7, 16–20 → 8, 21–30 → 9, 31–35 → 10 … 56–65 → 15;
  - Champion 1–2 → 1, 3 → 2, 4 → 3, 5 → 4, 6–7 → 5, 8–9 → 6, 10–11 → 7, 12–14 → 8, 15–17 → 9, 18 → 10, 19 → 11, 20–24 → 12, 25–26 → 13, 27–29 → 14, 30–40 → 15.
- Release history (wiki): 10 Dec 2023 announcement; Epic items released through events from Dec 2023 (Giant Gauntlet) to Aug 2026 (Revenge Deck); Dragon Duke items added Feb–Aug 2026.

## Client comparison

**Matches (every wiki level checked against the inherited client rows)**

- All 42 wiki items exist as non-deprecated `character_items.csv` records (renamed: Giant Arrow = `Piercing Arrow`, Frost Flake = `Frost Charm`, Electro Fangs = `ElectroAttack`, Revenge Deck = `Draconic Counter`)
- Equipment Migration template ranges = `heroes.csv` `MigrationGearLevel` (Barbarian King/Archer Queen 1–10→1 … 76–80→14; Grand Warden 1–3→1 … 51–55→14; Royal Champion 1–2→1 … 27–29→14; the client extends the final bracket →15 to every higher level)

**Client columns and interpretation notes**

- `character_items.csv`: one record per item, rows = levels; columns `Rarity`, `AllowedCharacters`, `RequiredBlacksmithLevel`, `RequiredCharacterLevel` (always 1), `UpgradeResources`/`UpgradeCosts` (next level), `MainAbilities`/`MainAbilityLevels`, `ExtraAbilities`/`ExtraAbilityLevels`, `HitPoints`, `DPS`, `AttackSpeedPercentage`, `HealOnActivation`, `Claimable` (Epic), `Deprecated`.
- `heroes.csv` `DefaultItems`/`ItemSlotCount` define starting loadouts and two slots; `MigrationGearLevel` encodes the migration table.

**Mismatches / ambiguities**

- None found in the compared fields.
