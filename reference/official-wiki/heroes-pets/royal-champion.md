# Royal Champion

- **Source:** https://clashofclans.fandom.com/wiki/Royal_Champion
- **Wiki revision:** 624597 (retrieved 2026-09-15 via the MediaWiki API)
- **Category:** hero
- **Client record:** `heroes.csv` → `Royal Champion`

> Defense-targeting, wall-jumping spear thrower unlocked at Hero Hall 7 (TH13); 3-tile range, Dark Elixir upgrades to level 55.

## Mechanics

- Unlock: Hero Hall 7 (TH13). Level caps: HH7/TH13 → 25, HH8/TH14 → 30, HH9/TH15 → 40, HH10/TH16 → 45, HH11/TH17 → 50, HH12/TH18 → 55. Upgrades cost Dark Elixir and a Builder.
- Combat: throws her spear every 1.2 s at 3 tiles, hits ground and air, jumps over Walls, wiki speed 24 (3 tiles/s), search radius 9 tiles.
- Targeting: preferred target Defenses — she bypasses other buildings for the nearest defense.
  - The Clan Castle never counts as a defense; an activated Town Hall weapon does.
  - If she becomes aware of defending heroes, Clan Castle troops or Skeleton Trap skeletons (hit by them, or near a friendly unit they attack) she switches to fight them, then returns to the nearest defense (or any building if no defenses remain).
- Ability: once per battle; restores 400 HP at levels 1–4, +100 every fifth level to 1,500 at level 55, plus equipment recovery; auto-activates on KO (setting).
- Equipment: starts with Seeking Shield and Royal Gem; Common unlocks Hog Rider Puppet (Blacksmith 7) and Haste Vial (Blacksmith 8); Epic: Rocket Spear, Electro Boots, Frost Flake. No equipment on defense.
- Defense: patrols her Hero Banner and retreats when lured too far.
- The old 30–48 minute 'regeneration time' stat was removed from gameplay in March 2025.

## Client comparison

**Matches (every wiki level checked against the inherited client rows)**

- Attack Speed `1.2s` = client `AttackSpeed 1200 ms`
- Movement Speed `24` = client `Speed 300 (÷12.5 = 24)`
- Range `3 tiles` = client `AttackRange 300 (= 3 tiles)`
- `damagePerSecond` = `heroes.DPS` (same value) (all 55 wiki rows)
- `damagePerHit` = client `DPS × AttackSpeed / 1000` (all 55 wiki rows)
- `hitpoints` = `heroes.Hitpoints` (same value) (all 55 wiki rows)
- `healthRecovery` = `SpecialAbilities:special_abilities[RoyalChampionAbilityHeal].HealOnActivation` (same value) (all 55 wiki rows)
- `upgradeCost` = client row N−1 `UpgradeCost` (the client stores the next upgrade on the current row) (all 55 wiki rows)
- `upgradeTimeSeconds` = client row N−1 `UpgradeTimeD/H/M` converted to seconds (next-upgrade convention) (all 55 wiki rows)
- `heroHallLevelRequired` = `heroes.RequiredHeroTavernLevel` (same value) (all 55 wiki rows)

**Client columns and interpretation notes**

- `heroes.csv` stores the *next* upgrade on each level row (`UpgradeCost`, `UpgradeResource`, `UpgradeTimeH`); the wiki lists the same price on the destination level. `RequiredTownHallLevel` and `RequiredHeroTavernLevel` on row N gate owning level N.
- Intrinsic activation recovery lives in `special_abilities.csv` (`<Hero>AbilityHeal`, column `HealOnActivation`) selected by the hero row's `SpecialAbilitiesLevel`; that ability has `ActiveAfterPlayerInput=TRUE`, `MaxActivations=1` (once per battle) and `SimulatePlayerInputOnDeath=TRUE` (the auto-use-on-KO behaviour). Equipment `HealOnActivation` is added on top.
- Units: `Speed` is internal speed (tiles/s × 100; wiki speed = Speed ÷ 12.5), `AttackRange` is 1/100 tile, `AttackSpeed` is milliseconds between attacks; damage per hit = `DPS × AttackSpeed / 1000`.
- `ItemSlotCount=2` and `DefaultItems` define the two equipment slots and the starting loadout; `MigrationGearLevel` is the equipment level granted by the December 2023 equipment migration (see hero-equipment.md).
- The wiki's 'Search Radius' has no one-to-one client column. Candidate defensive columns are `AlertRadius`, `MaxSearchRadiusForDefender` and `PatrolRadius` (all 1/100 tile); they are recorded under constants.client for implementers.
- `PreferedTargetBuildingClass=Defense`, `IsJumper=TRUE`, `NewTargetAttackDelay` 400 ms (extra delay before the first attack on a new target — not stated on the wiki), `ForceRetaliation=TRUE` (present on every hero, so it does not by itself explain her special retaliation rule).
- Defensive columns: `PatrolRadius` 300, `AlertRadius` 1200, `MaxSearchRadiusForDefender` 1000.

**Mismatches / ambiguities**

- `Search Radius`: wiki **9 tiles** vs client **AlertRadius 1200 / MaxSearchRadiusForDefender 1000 / PatrolRadius 300** — no direct client equivalent
