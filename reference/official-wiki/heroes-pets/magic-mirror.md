# Magic Mirror

- **Source:** https://clashofclans.fandom.com/wiki/Magic_Mirror
- **Wiki revision:** 621024 (retrieved 2026-09-15 via the MediaWiki API)
- **Category:** equipment · **Hero:** Archer Queen
- **Client record:** `character_items.csv` → `Magic Mirror`

> Queen Epic active item: summons 1 → 2 temporary Queen clones with fixed stats and makes her invisible for 1 s; passive HP and recovery.

## Mechanics

- Epic equipment for the Archer Queen; ability type **Active**; unlock/obtain: Buy in Anime Clash event for 3,100 Magic Medal; Buy in Dark Deal event for 3,100 Mischief Medal; Buy in Meltdown Mayhem event for 3,100 Ember Medal; Buy in Equipment Blast event for 3,100 Relic Medal; or purchasable from the Trader for 1,500 Gem.
- Levels 1–27. Blacksmith level required by equipment level: 1–12 → 1; 13–15 → 3; 16–18 → 5; 19–21 → 7; 22–24 → 8; 25–27 → 9.
- Total ore from level 1 to max (sum of wiki costs): 56,060 Shiny Ore, 3,720 Glowy Ore, 480 Starry Ore.
- Per-level stats (level 1 → max): Clones 1 → 2; Clone Duration 20 s → 30 s; Clone DPS 136 → 765; Clone Health 580 → 3,216; HP Recovery Increase 198 → 891; Hitpoint Increase 88 → 861.
- Ability: the Queen turns invisible for 1 s and summons clones in a semicircle behind her: 1 clone at levels 1–17, 2 at 18–27. Clones last 20 s (levels 1–8), 25 s (9–26) or 30 s (27), then vanish.
- Clone stats depend only on the mirror level (DPS 136 → 765, HP 580 → 3,216) although they display the Queen's level; they attack like the Queen (0.75 s).
- Clones get none of the effects of her other equipment, count as heroes for interactions (reduced spell/Healer effects, Headhunter preference, 25 housing for Recall), keep fighting after the real Queen dies, and ignore Angry Jelly brainwash.
- Recovery on activation +198 → +891 HP; passive +88 → +861 HP.
- Obtain: 3,100 event medals (Anime Clash, Dark Deal, Meltdown Mayhem, Equipment Blast) or 1,500 gems at the Trader.
