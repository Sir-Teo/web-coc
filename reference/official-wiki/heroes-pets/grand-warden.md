# Grand Warden

- **Source:** https://clashofclans.fandom.com/wiki/Grand_Warden
- **Wiki revision:** 625324 (retrieved 2026-09-15 via the MediaWiki API)

> Elixir-upgraded support hero (Hero Hall 5, TH11) with air/ground modes, 7-tile single-target attacks, a 9-tile aura ring and Warden-weight group targeting.

## Mechanics

- Unlock: Hero Hall 5 (TH11). He is the only Home Village hero upgraded with Elixir. Level caps: HH5/TH11 → 20, HH6/TH12 → 40, HH7/TH13 → 50, HH8/TH14 → 60, HH9/TH15 → 65, HH10/TH16 → 70, HH11/TH17 → 75, HH12/TH18 → 85.
- Combat: ranged single target, 7-tile range, attacks every 1.8 s, hits any target (ground/air), wiki speed 16 (2 tiles/s), search radius 10 tiles.
- Modes: selectable Air mode (flies) or Ground mode (walks). In both modes he passes over Walls, but he will hit a Wall when most nearby friendly housing is attacking it. On defense he always uses Ground mode.
- Targeting (Warden Weight):
  - he continuously sums the Warden Weight of valid friendly units within 13 tiles;
  - below a total of 20 he targets the building nearest to himself; at 20 or more he switches to group mode and locks onto the building attacked by the heaviest/most numerous nearby group, staying on it until destroyed;
  - weights (wiki template): most heroes 21, pets 3, Barbarian/Archer 0.5, Dragon 20, P.E.K.K.A 25, Golem 10, Lava Hound 9.5, Henchmen 8, Lavaloon (equipment) 15, Giant Giant 38.25, Snake 0.5, Booger 0.5, Frostmite 1, Apprentice Warden 0.
- Aura: equipment auras (Life Gem, Rage Gem) and ability areas (Eternal Tome, Healing Tome, Heroic Torch) use a ring centred on him (radius 9 tiles).
- Ability: once per battle; restores 90 HP at levels 1–4 rising to 285 at level 85, plus equipment recovery.
  - Auto-activates when he is about to be knocked out (setting); if a single hit would kill him (e.g. Seeking Air Mine) the ability still fires and he survives with 1 hitpoint.
- Equipment: starts with Eternal Tome and Life Gem; Common unlocks Rage Gem (Blacksmith 4) and Healing Tome (Blacksmith 6); Epic: Fireball, Lavaloon Puppet, Heroic Torch. No equipment (and no aura) on defense.

## Client comparison

**Matches (every wiki level checked against the inherited client rows)**

- Movement Speed `16` = client `Speed 200 (÷12.5 = 16)`
- Attack Speed `1.8s` = client `AttackSpeed 1800 ms`
- Range `7` = client `AttackRange 700 (= 7 tiles)`
- `damagePerSecond` = `heroes.DPS` (same value) (all 85 wiki rows)
- `damagePerHit` = client `DPS × AttackSpeed / 1000` (all 85 wiki rows)
- `hitpoints` = `heroes.Hitpoints` (same value) (all 85 wiki rows)
- `healthRecovery` = `SpecialAbilities:special_abilities[GrandWardenAbilityHeal].HealOnActivation` (same value) (all 85 wiki rows)
- `upgradeTimeSeconds` = client row N−1 `UpgradeTimeD/H/M` converted to seconds (next-upgrade convention) (all 85 wiki rows)
- `heroHallLevelRequired` = `heroes.RequiredHeroTavernLevel` (same value) (all 85 wiki rows)
- `Warden Weight template`: matches `FriendlyGroupWeight`/100 for every unit spot-checked (heroes 21, pets 3, Snake 0.5, Henchmen 8, Giant Giant 38.25, Lavaloon 15, Frostmite 1, Booger 0.5)

**Client columns and interpretation notes**

- `heroes.csv` stores the *next* upgrade on each level row (`UpgradeCost`, `UpgradeResource`, `UpgradeTimeH`); the wiki lists the same price on the destination level. `RequiredTownHallLevel` and `RequiredHeroTavernLevel` on row N gate owning level N.
- Intrinsic activation recovery lives in `special_abilities.csv` (`<Hero>AbilityHeal`, column `HealOnActivation`) selected by the hero row's `SpecialAbilitiesLevel`; that ability has `ActiveAfterPlayerInput=TRUE`, `MaxActivations=1` (once per battle) and `SimulatePlayerInputOnDeath=TRUE` (the auto-use-on-KO behaviour). Equipment `HealOnActivation` is added on top.
- Units: `Speed` is internal speed (tiles/s × 100; wiki speed = Speed ÷ 12.5), `AttackRange` is 1/100 tile, `AttackSpeed` is milliseconds between attacks; damage per hit = `DPS × AttackSpeed / 1000`.
- `ItemSlotCount=2` and `DefaultItems` define the two equipment slots and the starting loadout; `MigrationGearLevel` is the equipment level granted by the December 2023 equipment migration (see hero-equipment.md).
- The wiki's 'Search Radius' has no one-to-one client column. Candidate defensive columns are `AlertRadius`, `MaxSearchRadiusForDefender` and `PatrolRadius` (all 1/100 tile); they are recorded under constants.client for implementers.
- `UpgradeResource=Elixir`. `HasAltMode=TRUE`, `AltModeFlying=TRUE`, `IsJumper=TRUE` (wall bypass), `AltAttackRange` 650 and `CoolDownOverride` 1050 are extra mode/attack-cycle columns the wiki does not describe.
- Group targeting: `FightWithGroups=TRUE`, `TargetGroupsRange` 1300 (= 13-tile vision), `TargetGroupsMinWeight` 2000 (= weight 20), `TargetGroupsRadius` 500. Each unit's Warden weight is its `FriendlyGroupWeight` ÷ 100 (heroes 2100 → 21, pets 300 → 3, Snake/Booger 50 → 0.5, Henchman 800 → 8, Giant Giant 3825 → 38.25, equipment Lavaloon 1500 → 15, Frostmite 100 → 1); the Warden's own `FriendlyGroupWeight` is 0. Global `WARDEN_WAITS_FOR_GROUP_RETARGETING` also applies.
- Aura ring: `AuraSpell=GrandWardenRangeRing` (spells.csv `Radius` 900 = 9 tiles, pulses every 1000 ms).
- Wall behaviour on the wiki ('attacks a Wall if most nearby housing does') has no single column; it follows from group targeting.
