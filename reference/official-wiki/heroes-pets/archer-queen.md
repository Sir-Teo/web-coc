# Archer Queen

- **Source:** https://clashofclans.fandom.com/wiki/Archer_Queen
- **Wiki revision:** 625026 (retrieved 2026-09-15 via the MediaWiki API)
- **Category:** hero
- **Client record:** `heroes.csv` → `Archer Queen`

> Ranged ground-and-air hero unlocked at Hero Hall 2 (TH8); 5-tile single-target attacks, Dark Elixir upgrades to level 110.

## Mechanics

- Unlock: Hero Hall level 2 (requires TH8). Level caps: HH2/TH8 → 10, HH3/TH9 → 30, HH4/TH10 → 40, HH5/TH11 → 50, HH6/TH12 → 65, HH7/TH13 → 75, HH8/TH14 → 85, HH9/TH15 → 90, HH10/TH16 → 95, HH11/TH17 → 100, HH12/TH18 → 110.
- Combat: ranged single-target attack every 0.75 s at 5 tiles (1.5 tiles more than an Archer), hits ground and air, no preferred target; wiki movement speed 24 (3 tiles/s); search radius 10 tiles. The three arrows per shot are cosmetic — damage applies once to one target.
- Ability: once per battle; restores a flat amount (115 at levels 1–4, +15 every fifth level to 445 at level 110) plus equipment recovery, and triggers both equipped items. Auto-activates on KO if unused (setting).
- Equipment: starts with Archer Puppet and Invisibility Vial; Common unlocks Giant Arrow (Blacksmith 2) and Healer Puppet (Blacksmith 5); Epic: Frozen Arrow, Magic Mirror, Action Figure, Monolith Arrow. No equipment on defense.
- Defense: assigned to a Hero Banner; patrols its area, retreats when lured too far, can shoot air units (useful against air raids). Not required for 3 stars.
- Upgrading uses a Builder; she is unavailable for attacks while upgrading.

## Client comparison

**Matches (every wiki level checked against the inherited client rows)**

- Movement Speed `24` = client `Speed 300 (÷12.5 = 24)`
- Attack Speed `0.75s` = client `AttackSpeed 750 ms`
- Range `5 tiles` = client `AttackRange 500 (= 5 tiles)`
- `damagePerSecond` = `heroes.DPS` (same value) (all 110 wiki rows)
- `damagePerHit` = client `DPS × AttackSpeed / 1000` (all 110 wiki rows)
- `hitpoints` = `heroes.Hitpoints` (same value) (all 110 wiki rows)
- `healthRecovery` = `SpecialAbilities:special_abilities[ArcherQueenAbilityHeal].HealOnActivation` (same value) (all 110 wiki rows)
- `upgradeCost` = client row N−1 `UpgradeCost` (the client stores the next upgrade on the current row) (all 110 wiki rows)
- `upgradeTimeSeconds` = client row N−1 `UpgradeTimeD/H/M` converted to seconds (next-upgrade convention) (all 110 wiki rows)
- `heroHallLevelRequired` = `heroes.RequiredHeroTavernLevel` (same value) (all 110 wiki rows)

**Client columns and interpretation notes**

- `heroes.csv` stores the *next* upgrade on each level row (`UpgradeCost`, `UpgradeResource`, `UpgradeTimeH`); the wiki lists the same price on the destination level. `RequiredTownHallLevel` and `RequiredHeroTavernLevel` on row N gate owning level N.
- Intrinsic activation recovery lives in `special_abilities.csv` (`<Hero>AbilityHeal`, column `HealOnActivation`) selected by the hero row's `SpecialAbilitiesLevel`; that ability has `ActiveAfterPlayerInput=TRUE`, `MaxActivations=1` (once per battle) and `SimulatePlayerInputOnDeath=TRUE` (the auto-use-on-KO behaviour). Equipment `HealOnActivation` is added on top.
- Units: `Speed` is internal speed (tiles/s × 100; wiki speed = Speed ÷ 12.5), `AttackRange` is 1/100 tile, `AttackSpeed` is milliseconds between attacks; damage per hit = `DPS × AttackSpeed / 1000`.
- `ItemSlotCount=2` and `DefaultItems` define the two equipment slots and the starting loadout; `MigrationGearLevel` is the equipment level granted by the December 2023 equipment migration (see hero-equipment.md).
- The wiki's 'Search Radius' has no one-to-one client column. Candidate defensive columns are `AlertRadius`, `MaxSearchRadiusForDefender` and `PatrolRadius` (all 1/100 tile); they are recorded under constants.client for implementers.
- Projectile changes with level (`Projectile` varies between `ArcherQueen arrow lvl*` records) — visual only.
- Defensive columns: `PatrolRadius` 300, `AlertRadius` 1300, `MaxSearchRadiusForDefender` 1000.

**Mismatches / ambiguities**

- `Search Radius`: wiki **10 tiles** vs client **AlertRadius 1300 / MaxSearchRadiusForDefender 1000 / PatrolRadius 300** — no direct client equivalent
