# Ore Calculations

- **Source:** https://clashofclans.fandom.com/wiki/Ore_Calculations
- **Wiki revision:** 610468 (retrieved 2026-09-15 via the MediaWiki API)
- **Category:** mechanic
- **Client record:** none (see Client comparison)

> Community (user-written) calculation page: relative ore values from war/Star Bonus/gem/medal prices and cumulative equipment costs; not official data.

## Mechanics

- Non-authoritative: the page is an editor's analysis ('feel free to comment or edit'); its war and league tables use an older reward structure (Bronze…Legends leagues).
- Cumulative cost to max — Common: 27,260 Shiny, 1,920 Glowy; Epic: 56,060 Shiny, 3,720 Glowy, 480 Starry (identical to the per-item wiki tables and client `UpgradeCosts`).
- Stated gem ratios: 1 gem ≈ 2 Shiny ≈ 0.4 Glowy ≈ 0.0545 Starry (i.e. Starry valued at ~18.3 gems) — this contradicts the Blacksmith gem price of 1/5/35 per ore and should not be used for the in-game shortfall price.
- Raid-medal ratios derive from the Trader deals (500 Shiny/350, 50 Glowy/300, 5 Starry/350 medals).

## Client comparison

**Client columns and interpretation notes**

- Equipment cost scaling table = `character_items.csv` `UpgradeCosts` sequence (checked on every item page).
- Gem value ratios are editorial and have no client counterpart; the client gem prices are `globals` COMMON_ORE_DIAMOND_COST_* / RARE_ORE_DIAMOND_COST 5 / EPIC_ORE_DIAMOND_COST 35.

**Mismatches / ambiguities**

- `gemValueRatio`: wiki **1 gem = 2 Shiny / 0.4 Glowy / 0.0545 Starry (community estimate)** vs client **1 Shiny = 1 gem, 1 Glowy = 5 gems, 1 Starry = 35 gems** — editorial valuation vs in-game shortfall price
