# Barbarian King

- **Source:** https://clashofclans.fandom.com/wiki/Barbarian_King
- **Wiki revision:** 623671 (retrieved 2026-09-15 via the MediaWiki API)
- **Category:** hero
- **Client record:** `heroes.csv` → `Barbarian King`

> First Home Village hero: melee ground-only tank unlocked with Hero Hall 1 at TH4, upgraded with Dark Elixir from TH7 to level 110.

## Mechanics

- Unlock: completing the Hero Hall (level 1, Town Hall 4) unlocks him at level 1. Level 1 itself costs nothing; every later level costs Dark Elixir and needs a free Builder, and he cannot attack (or defend in normal multiplayer) while upgrading.
- Level caps by Hero Hall / Town Hall: HH1 → 10 (but TH4–6 stay at level 1; upgrades begin at TH7), HH2/TH8 → 20, HH3/TH9 → 30, HH4/TH10 → 40, HH5/TH11 → 50, HH6/TH12 → 65, HH7/TH13 → 75, HH8/TH14 → 85, HH9/TH15 → 90, HH10/TH16 → 95, HH11/TH17 → 100, HH12/TH18 → 110.
- Town Hall 4–6 'limited' King:
  - he cannot be upgraded, his equipment cannot be changed and he cannot defend (no Hero Banner before TH7);
  - his level-1 DPS, hitpoints and health recovery — and the passive boosts of the default Barbarian Puppet/Rage Vial — are scaled to 50% at TH4, 75% at TH5 and 100% at TH6;
  - ability attributes of the default equipment (summon count, rage %, speed boosts, durations) are not scaled. The wiki level table therefore shows level 1 three times (TH4, TH5, TH6).
- Combat: melee (1-tile range), attacks every 1.2 s, ground targets only, no preferred target; wiki movement speed 16 (= 2 tiles/s). Wiki search radius 9 tiles. He is the only melee, ground-only Home Village hero.
- Hero slot rules (Hero Hall): one slot at HH1, a second at HH3 (TH9), third at HH5 (TH11), fourth at HH7 (TH13). At TH8 only one of King/Queen can be taken to attack; attack and defense assignments are independent.
- Ability: one activation per battle. Activating always restores a flat amount of health (health recovery stat: 200 at levels 1–4, rising every fifth level to 3,250 at level 110) and fires the active effects of both equipped items; recovery from equipment adds to it.
  - If the ability is still unused when he would be knocked out, it fires automatically (player setting 'Automatically use Hero Abilities on KO').
  - Equipping two passive items still leaves an activatable ability consisting only of the recovery.
- Equipment: two slots. Starts with Barbarian Puppet and Rage Vial; Earthquake Boots (Blacksmith 1) and Vampstache (Blacksmith 3) are Common unlocks; Giant Gauntlet, Spiky Ball, Snake Bracelet and Stick Horse are Epic (event medals / Trader gems). Equipment is not used on defense.
- Defense: from TH7 he is assigned to a Hero Banner, patrols around it, chases a locked troop until it or he dies, and walks back if lured outside his patrol zone. Defending heroes are never required for a 3-star result.
- Regeneration: heroes no longer need to regenerate between battles; each attack starts at full health with the ability available (Hero Hall/Royal Champion pages; see heroes.md).

## Client comparison

**Matches (every wiki level checked against the inherited client rows)**

- Movement Speed `16` = client `Speed 200 (÷12.5 = 16)`
- Attack Speed `1.2s` = client `AttackSpeed 1200 ms`
- Range `1 tile` = client `AttackRange 100 (= 1 tiles)`
- `damagePerSecond` = `heroes.DPS` (same value) (all 112 wiki rows)
- `damagePerHit` = client `DPS × AttackSpeed / 1000` (all 112 wiki rows)
- `hitpoints` = `heroes.Hitpoints` (same value) (all 112 wiki rows)
- `healthRecovery` = `SpecialAbilities:special_abilities[BarbarianKingAbilityHeal].HealOnActivation` (same value) (all 112 wiki rows)
- `upgradeTimeSeconds` = client row N−1 `UpgradeTimeD/H/M` converted to seconds (next-upgrade convention) (all 112 wiki rows)
- `heroHallLevelRequired` = `heroes.RequiredHeroTavernLevel` (same value) (all 112 wiki rows)
- `townHallLevelRequired`: levels 2–110 equal `heroes.RequiredTownHallLevel`; the wiki lists level 1 once each for TH4/TH5/TH6 while the client gates level 1 at TH4 (`RequiredTownHallLevel` 4)
- Level 1 at TH4: DPS/hitpoints/recovery = client level-1 values × `townhall_levels.ScaleByTHPercent` 50% (51 / 722.5 / 100)
- Level 1 at TH5: DPS/hitpoints/recovery = client level-1 values × `townhall_levels.ScaleByTHPercent` 75% (76.5 / 1,083.75 / 150)

**Client columns and interpretation notes**

- `heroes.csv` stores the *next* upgrade on each level row (`UpgradeCost`, `UpgradeResource`, `UpgradeTimeH`); the wiki lists the same price on the destination level. `RequiredTownHallLevel` and `RequiredHeroTavernLevel` on row N gate owning level N.
- Intrinsic activation recovery lives in `special_abilities.csv` (`<Hero>AbilityHeal`, column `HealOnActivation`) selected by the hero row's `SpecialAbilitiesLevel`; that ability has `ActiveAfterPlayerInput=TRUE`, `MaxActivations=1` (once per battle) and `SimulatePlayerInputOnDeath=TRUE` (the auto-use-on-KO behaviour). Equipment `HealOnActivation` is added on top.
- Units: `Speed` is internal speed (tiles/s × 100; wiki speed = Speed ÷ 12.5), `AttackRange` is 1/100 tile, `AttackSpeed` is milliseconds between attacks; damage per hit = `DPS × AttackSpeed / 1000`.
- `ItemSlotCount=2` and `DefaultItems` define the two equipment slots and the starting loadout; `MigrationGearLevel` is the equipment level granted by the December 2023 equipment migration (see hero-equipment.md).
- The wiki's 'Search Radius' has no one-to-one client column. Candidate defensive columns are `AlertRadius`, `MaxSearchRadiusForDefender` and `PatrolRadius` (all 1/100 tile); they are recorded under constants.client for implementers.
- `ScaleByTH=TRUE` is set only on the Barbarian King; the percentages come from `townhall_levels.csv` `ScaleByTHPercent` (TH4 50, TH5 75, TH6 100). The three wiki level-1 rows (722.5/1,083.75/1,445 HP etc.) are exactly 50/75/100% of the client level-1 row.
- Recovery tiers: `SpecialAbilitiesLevel` changes at levels 5, 10, 15 … 110 (23 tiers, 200 → 3,250).
- Defensive columns: `PatrolRadius` 300, `AlertRadius` 1200, `MaxSearchRadiusForDefender` 1000, `HousingSpace` 25 (hero housing used by spells/counters, not army camp space).
