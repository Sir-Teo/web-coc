# Lavaloon Puppet

- **Wiki revision:** 623290 (retrieved 2026-09-15 via the MediaWiki API)
- **Category:** equipment · **Hero:** Grand Warden

- **Also used:** [Lavaloon Puppet/Lavaloon](https://clashofclans.fandom.com/wiki/Lavaloon_Puppet/Lavaloon) (revision 620198)

> Warden Epic active item: summons 1 → 3 weakened Lavaloons (level 1 → 11) that hunt Air Defenses, bomb as they fly and burst into pups; passive DPS and HP.

## Mechanics

- Epic equipment for the Grand Warden; ability type **Active**; unlock/obtain: Buy in Mashup Mania event for 3,100 Mashup Medal; Buy in Friend or Foe event for 3,100 Champ Medal; Buy in Meltdown Mayhem event for 3,100 Ember Medal; or purchasable from the Trader for 1,500 Gem.
- Levels 1–27. Blacksmith level required by equipment level: 1–12 → 1; 13–15 → 3; 16–18 → 5; 19–21 → 7; 22–24 → 8; 25–27 → 9.
- Total ore from level 1 to max (sum of wiki costs): 56,060 Shiny Ore, 3,720 Glowy Ore, 480 Starry Ore.
- Per-level stats (level 1 → max): Summoned Lavaloons 1 → 3; Lavaloon Level 1 → 11; Damage per Second Increase 10 → 50; Hitpoint Increase 50 → 150.
- Ability: summons Lavaloons one at a time: 1 (levels 1–8), 2 (9–20), 3 (21–27). Lavaloon level rises every three item levels up to level 20, then every two (8 at 21–22, 9 at 23–24, 10 at 25–26, 11 at 27) — so the level-24 upgrade does not improve the ability.
- Lavaloon: flying, prefers Air Defenses, 21 housing, wiki speed 16; while flying toward its target it drops bombs (ground splash) every 2.5 s, each spawning a Lavaloon Pup.
  - On death it explodes (180 → 1,035 damage) and releases 1 → 5 pups; stats are about 10% below the event Lavaloon of the same level (970 → 4,590 HP, 45 → 202 DPS).
- Passive: +10 → +50 DPS and +50 → +150 HP.
- Obtain: 3,100 event medals (Mashup Mania, Friend or Foe, Meltdown Mayhem) or 1,500 gems at the Trader.
