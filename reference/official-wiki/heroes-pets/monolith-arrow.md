# Monolith Arrow

- **Source:** https://clashofclans.fandom.com/wiki/Monolith_Arrow
- **Wiki revision:** 625282 (retrieved 2026-09-15 via the MediaWiki API)
- **Category:** equipment · **Hero:** Archer Queen
- **Client record:** `character_items.csv` → `Monolith Arrow`

> Queen Epic passive item: attacks deal bonus damage equal to a % of the target's max HP (9.5% → 14%, reduced after 180 and 250 housing deployed) plus 3% → 10% damage reduction; passive HP.

## Mechanics

- Epic equipment for the Archer Queen; ability type **Passive**; unlock/obtain: Buy in Anime Fury Medal Event for 3,100 Meow Medal; or purchasable from the Trader for 1,500 Gem.
- Levels 1–27. Blacksmith level required by equipment level: 1–12 → 1; 13–15 → 3; 16–18 → 5; 19–21 → 7; 22–24 → 8; 25–27 → 9.
- Total ore from level 1 to max (sum of wiki costs): 56,060 Shiny Ore, 3,720 Glowy Ore, 480 Starry Ore.
- Per-level stats (level 1 → max): Highest Extra Damage (Below 180 Housing Space) 9.5% → 14%; Extra Damage (181-250 Housing Space) 7% → 10%; Extra Damage (251+ Housing Space) 4% → 5%; Incoming Damage Reduction 3% → 10%; Hitpoint Increase 100 → 1,160.
- Passive ability: each Queen attack adds damage equal to a percentage of the target's maximum hitpoints. Three stages by total housing space the attacker has deployed: ≤180 → 9.5% (level 1) … 14% (27); 181–250 → 7% … 10%; 251+ → 4% … 5%. A stage changes only when the threshold is exceeded (exactly 180/250 keeps the higher stage).
- The Queen also takes 3% less damage (levels 1–8), rising by 1% per tier from level 9 to 10% at 27.
- Housing counter: counts all deployed housing whether or not the Queen is on the field and keeps counting after she falls.
  - Heroes (including her) add 25; own Siege Machines add 1 (Clan Castle sieges do not); pets, spells, Clan Castle troops and subtroops (Skeletons, Big Boy, Siege Barracks units) add nothing.
- The bonus damage is not modified by Rage Spells or difficulty modifiers.
- Passive: +100 → +1,160 HP.
- Before the June 2026 update the effect switched off entirely (including the damage reduction) above 460 housing.
- Obtain: Anime Fury Medal Event (3,100 Meow Medals) or 1,500 gems at the Trader.

**Client columns and interpretation notes**

- Three abilities implement the stages: `ArcherQueenMonolithArrow` (`DamagePermilHp` 95 → 140, `DeactivateAfterHousingSpaceDeployed` 181, projectile `ArcherQueenMonolithProjectile3`), `ExtraAbilities` `ArcherQueenMonolithArrowMid` (70 → 100, deactivates at 251) and `ArcherQueenMonolithArrowMin` (40 → 50, never deactivates). `InfoScreenAttribute1/2` 180/250 are UI thresholds.
- All three rows carry `ShieldProtectionPercent` 3 → 10; the wiki describes a single reduction, so the engine presumably does not stack them.

**Mismatches / ambiguities**

- `damageReductionStacking`: wiki **one 3–10% reduction** vs client **ShieldProtectionPercent on all three simultaneously active stage abilities** — verify that shields from concurrent abilities do not add
- `stageSelection`: wiki **only one stage applies (9.5% at ≤180 housing)** vs client **Main+Mid+Min abilities are all active below 181 housing (only Main/Mid carry DeactivateAfterHousingSpaceDeployed)** — engine must pick the highest active stage rather than summing DamagePermilHp
