# Minion Prince

- **Source:** https://clashofclans.fandom.com/wiki/Minion_Prince
- **Wiki revision:** 625353 (retrieved 2026-09-15 via the MediaWiki API)
- **Category:** hero
- **Client record:** `heroes.csv` → `Minion Prince`

> Flying ranged hero unlocked at Hero Hall 3 (TH9); hits ground and air from 4.5 tiles, Dark Elixir upgrades to level 95.

## Mechanics

- Unlock: Hero Hall 3 (TH9). Level caps: HH3/TH9 → 10, HH4/TH10 → 20, HH5/TH11 → 30, HH6/TH12 → 40, HH7/TH13 → 50, HH8/TH14 → 60, HH9/TH15 → 70, HH10/TH16 → 80, HH11/TH17 → 90, HH12/TH18 → 95.
- Combat: air unit; ranged attacks every 0.85 s at 4.5 tiles against ground and air, any target; wiki speed 24 (3 tiles/s); search radius 10 tiles. On defense he also flies, so ground-only attackers cannot reach him.
- Ability: once per battle; restores 200 HP at levels 1–4, +40 every fifth level to 1,050 at level 95, plus equipment recovery. Auto-activates on KO (setting) and, like the Warden, survives a one-shot with 1 HP when it fires.
- Equipment: starts with Henchmen Puppet and Dark Orb; Common unlocks Metal Pants (Blacksmith 3) and Noble Iron (Blacksmith 5); Epic: Dark Crown, Meteor Staff. No equipment on defense.
- Starting level after the TH17 migration:
  - a player given Hero Hall N (N ≥ 4) received a Minion Prince at the level cap of Hero Hall N−1 (HH5 → level 20, HH10 → level 70); a Hero Hall 3 gives level 1.

## Client comparison

**Matches (every wiki level checked against the inherited client rows)**

- Movement Speed `24` = client `Speed 300 (÷12.5 = 24)`
- Attack Speed `0.85s` = client `AttackSpeed 850 ms`
- Range `4.5 tiles` = client `AttackRange 450 (= 4.5 tiles)`
- `damagePerSecond` = `heroes.DPS` (same value) (all 95 wiki rows)
- `damagePerHit` = client `DPS × AttackSpeed / 1000` (all 95 wiki rows)
- `hitpoints` = `heroes.Hitpoints` (same value) (all 95 wiki rows)
- `upgradeCost` = client row N−1 `UpgradeCost` (the client stores the next upgrade on the current row) (all 95 wiki rows)
- `upgradeTimeSeconds` = client row N−1 `UpgradeTimeD/H/M` converted to seconds (next-upgrade convention) (all 95 wiki rows)
- `heroHallLevelRequired` = `heroes.RequiredHeroTavernLevel` (same value) (all 95 wiki rows)

**Client columns and interpretation notes**

- `heroes.csv` stores the *next* upgrade on each level row (`UpgradeCost`, `UpgradeResource`, `UpgradeTimeH`); the wiki lists the same price on the destination level. `RequiredTownHallLevel` and `RequiredHeroTavernLevel` on row N gate owning level N.
- Intrinsic activation recovery lives in `special_abilities.csv` (`<Hero>AbilityHeal`, column `HealOnActivation`) selected by the hero row's `SpecialAbilitiesLevel`; that ability has `ActiveAfterPlayerInput=TRUE`, `MaxActivations=1` (once per battle) and `SimulatePlayerInputOnDeath=TRUE` (the auto-use-on-KO behaviour). Equipment `HealOnActivation` is added on top.
- Units: `Speed` is internal speed (tiles/s × 100; wiki speed = Speed ÷ 12.5), `AttackRange` is 1/100 tile, `AttackSpeed` is milliseconds between attacks; damage per hit = `DPS × AttackSpeed / 1000`.
- `ItemSlotCount=2` and `DefaultItems` define the two equipment slots and the starting loadout; `MigrationGearLevel` is the equipment level granted by the December 2023 equipment migration (see hero-equipment.md).
- The wiki's 'Search Radius' has no one-to-one client column. Candidate defensive columns are `AlertRadius`, `MaxSearchRadiusForDefender` and `PatrolRadius` (all 1/100 tile); they are recorded under constants.client for implementers.
- `IsFlying=TRUE`, `NewTargetAttackDelay` 450 ms, `TargetedEffectOffset` 80.
- `LevelGrantedByTavernLevel` encodes the migration: levels 1–9 → 3, 10–19 → 4, 20–29 → 5 … 80–90 → 11, 91–95 → 100. Interpreting the granted level as the first level tagged with the player's Hero Hall reproduces the wiki rule (HH4 → 10, HH5 → 20, HH10 → 70).

**Mismatches / ambiguities**

- `healthRecovery` (level 22): wiki **175** vs client **360** — closest client column `SpecialAbilities:special_abilities[MinionPrinceAbilityHeal].HealOnActivation` matches 94 other levels
- `Search Radius`: wiki **10 tiles** vs client **AlertRadius 1200 / MaxSearchRadiusForDefender 1000 / PatrolRadius 300** — no direct client equivalent
