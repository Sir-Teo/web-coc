# Dark Crown

- **Source:** https://clashofclans.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Crown
- **Wiki revision:** 624599 (retrieved 2026-09-15 via the MediaWiki API)
- **Category:** equipment · **Hero:** Minion Prince
- **Client record:** `character_items.csv` → `Dark Crown`

> Prince Epic passive item: gains up to three stacking-tier buffs (+10/20/30% HP and damage at max) as 60/120/180 housing of his allies die; passive HP and attack speed.

## Mechanics

- Epic equipment for the Minion Prince; ability type **Passive**; unlock/obtain: Buy in Dark Deal event for 3,100 Mischief Medal; Buy in Meltdown Mayhem event for 3,100 Ember Medal; Buy in Equipment Blast event for 3,100 Relic Medal; or purchasable from the Trader for 1,500 Gem.
- Levels 1–27. Blacksmith level required by equipment level: 1–12 → 1; 13–15 → 3; 16–18 → 5; 19–21 → 7; 22–24 → 8; 25–27 → 9.
- Total ore from level 1 to max (sum of wiki costs): 56,060 Shiny Ore, 3,720 Glowy Ore, 480 Starry Ore.
- Per-level stats (level 1 → max): First Health & Damage Increase 1% → 10%; Second Health & Damage Increase 2% → 20%; Max Health Increase 3% → 30%; Max Damage Increase 3% → 30%; Hitpoint Increase 550 → 1,200; Attack Speed Increase 1% → 11%.
- Passive ability: while the Prince is in the battle, the housing space of friendly units that die is counted. At 60, 120 and 180 counted housing he reaches tier 1, 2 and 3: max HP and damage +1%/+2%/+3% at level 1 up to +10%/+20%/+30% at level 27; each tier replaces the previous one, and he grows slightly (about 5% per tier).
- What counts: all attacking troops that die anywhere on the map, including self-destructing troops (Wall Breakers, Yetimites), subtroops (Skeletons, Big Boy, Siege Barracks units), units summoned by spells (Skeleton/Bat/Totem spells, Bag of Frostmites), pets (20 each) and other heroes (25 each, again after revives).
  - What does not: defending units, spells themselves, Siege Machines, cloned troops (including Magic Mirror Queens), the Prince himself, units dying while he is recalled; a Druid that turns into a Bear by surviving adds nothing.
  - The counter keeps running while he is knocked out (benefits after a revive).
- The tier boosts are buffs, not base stats: a stronger Rage Spell overrides the damage buff, and they do not stack with an Apprentice Warden life aura.
- Passive: +550 → +1,200 HP and +1% → +11% attack speed (interval ≈ 0.842 s → 0.766 s).
- Obtain: 3,100 event medals (Dark Deal, Meltdown Mayhem, Equipment Blast) or 1,500 gems at the Trader. Its release came with housing-space cuts for many subtroops (e.g. Lava Pup 2 → 1, Big Boy 20 → 10, Bear 16 → 10).

## Client comparison

**Matches (every wiki level checked against the inherited client rows)**

- `hitpointIncrease` = `character_items.HitPoints` (all levels)
- `attackSpeedIncreasePercent` = `character_items.AttackSpeedPercentage` (all levels)
- `blacksmithLevelRequired` = `character_items.RequiredBlacksmithLevel` (all levels)
- Ore costs: the wiki cost on level N equals client row N−1 `UpgradeResources`/`UpgradeCosts` (CommonOre = Shiny, RareOre = Glowy, EpicOre = Starry) for every level
- `firstHealthAndDamageIncreasePercent` = `ExtraAbilities:special_abilities[MinionDarkCrownAbilityLowestTier].PercentHealthIncrease` at the item's `MainAbilityLevels`/`ExtraAbilityLevels` (same value)
- `secondHealthAndDamageIncreasePercent` = `ExtraAbilities:special_abilities[MinionDarkCrownAbilityMediumTier].PercentHealthIncrease` at the item's `MainAbilityLevels`/`ExtraAbilityLevels` (same value)
- `maxHealthIncreasePercent` = `MainAbilities:special_abilities[MinionDarkCrownAbilityHighestTier].PercentHealthIncrease` at the item's `MainAbilityLevels`/`ExtraAbilityLevels` (same value)
- `maxDamageIncreasePercent` = `MainAbilities:special_abilities[MinionDarkCrownAbilityHighestTier].BoostDamagePercentage` at the item's `MainAbilityLevels`/`ExtraAbilityLevels` (same value)

**Client columns and interpretation notes**

- Three passive abilities keyed to `ActiveAfterFriendlyHousingSpaceDied`: `MinionDarkCrownAbilityLowestTier` 60 (`GrowthScale` 4), `MinionDarkCrownAbilityMediumTier` 120 (`GrowthScale` 8) and `MinionDarkCrownAbilityHighestTier` 180 (`GrowthScale` 12, the item's `MainAbilities`); each sets equal `PercentHealthIncrease` and `BoostDamagePercentage`. `MinionDarkCrownAbilityConstantAura` has no data columns (visual aura).
- Pet and hero housing used by the counter come from `pets.HousingSpace` 20 and `heroes.HousingSpace` 25.

**Mismatches / ambiguities**

- `growth`: wiki **about 5% larger per tier (max ≈ 15%)** vs client **GrowthScale 4 / 8 / 12** — client growth is 4/8/12 (units presumably %)
