# Hero's Journey

- **Source:** https://clashofclans.fandom.com/wiki/Hero's_Journey
- **Wiki revision:** 625220 (retrieved 2026-09-15 via the MediaWiki API)
- **Category:** mechanic
- **Client record:** none (see Client comparison)

> TH7+ Hero Hall reward track driven by cumulative Home Village hero levels, with hero quests, Epic equipment rewards and TH-scaled Ore Chests.

## Mechanics

- Available from Town Hall 7 as a tab in the Hero Hall. Progress is the sum of all Home Village hero levels (Builder Base heroes excluded); tiers unlock at fixed cumulative totals (2 … 480).
- Unclaimed rewards wait 7 days then auto-claim (or auto-sell if storage is full); equipment and skins wait indefinitely.
- Hero Quests: score 15 stars in multiplayer within 14 days using the named hero or equipment; success gives three Ore Chests (one Shiny, one Glowy, one Starry).
  - A failed quest can be resumed once for gems, with a new 14-day timer.
  - If the required Epic item is not owned, the quest falls back to that hero's starting item, switching back if the Epic is obtained mid-quest.
- Equipment reward tiers give the first not-yet-owned Epic item from a fixed per-hero list (King: Giant Gauntlet, Spiky Ball, Snake Bracelet, Stick Horse; Queen: Frozen Arrow, Magic Mirror, Action Figure; Prince: Dark Crown, Meteor Staff; Warden: Fireball, Lavaloon Puppet, Heroic Torch; Champion: Rocket Spear, Electro Boots, Frost Flake; Duke: Rocket Backpack) at the level stated (1, 6, 9, 12 or 15); if all are owned the reward becomes 50 Starry Ore.
- Ore Chest contents scale with Town Hall (TH8: 250–350 Shiny, 9–19 Glowy, 1–3 Starry … TH18: 1,950–2,150 / 90–110 / 50–60); players far behind in the equipment economy get the larger 'accelerated' ranges.
- On release, TH7+ players were placed mid-track according to their hero levels and received the skipped rewards.
