## Mechanics

- Unlocked at Town Hall 2; the first defense that can hit air units. Count (wiki): TH2 1, TH4 2, TH5 3, TH7 4, TH8 5, TH9 6, TH10 7, TH11 8, TH17 9. When all Multi-Archer Towers (and at TH17 the Multi-Gear Tower) are built, 4 (TH16) / 2 (TH17) standalone towers remain.
- Footprint 3x3. An Archer model stands on top (visual only).
- Long Range (normal) mode: range 10 tiles, attack every 0.5 s, single target, targets **ground and air**. Damage per shot = DPS x 0.5 (level 21: 145 DPS / 72.5 per shot).
- Gear Up (Fast Attack mode): from level 10 (TH8) via the Master Builder once a Builder Base Archer Tower is level 6 (BH6); costs 3,000,000 Gold and 7 days. Fire interval halves to 0.25 s (DPS doubles, per-shot damage unchanged at most levels) and range falls to 8 tiles (raised from 7 on Feb 23, 2026). Permanent once geared up.
- Merging: at TH16 two non-geared level 21 Archer Towers merge into a Multi-Archer Tower; at TH17 a geared-up level 21 Archer Tower plus a geared-up level 21 Cannon merge into the Multi-Gear Tower. No further levels or Supercharges for the Archer Tower.

### Recent balance notes (from the page's History table)

- February 23, 2026: geared-up Archer Tower range 7 -> 8 tiles.
- October 6, 2025 and March 24, 2025: build time and cost reductions (Gear Up 4M -> 3M).

**Interpretation of client columns**

- `DPS` normal; per shot = `DPS` x `AttackSpeed` 500/1000. `AttackRange` 1000 = 10 tiles, air and ground targets.
- Fast Attack: `AltDPS` (about 2 x DPS), `AltAttackSpeed` 250 ms, `AltAttackRange` 800 = 8 tiles (already reflects the Feb 2026 range buff). Per shot = `AltDPS` x 0.25 matches the wiki's fast-attack damage per shot for all levels 10-21. Levels 12-21 per-shot values (37.5, 40, 45 ...) differ slightly from normal mode because AltDPS is not exactly 2x DPS there.
- `DefenderCharacter`/`DefenderCount` put the Archer model on top; it has no gameplay stats of its own on the tower.
- Gear Up: `GearUpBuilding` = BB Archer Tower, `GearUpLevelRequirement` 5 (0-based -> level 6), `GearUpCost` 3,000,000 and `GearUpTime` 10080 minutes (7 days) on the level 10 row.
