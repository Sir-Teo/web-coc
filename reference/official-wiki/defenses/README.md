# Official wiki cross-reference: Home Village defenses, traps and Town Hall weapons

This folder pairs the official Clash of Clans Wiki (clashofclans.fandom.com) with the pinned client **18.400.21** logic tables for every Home Village defense, trap and Town Hall weapon. All pages were retrieved on **2026-09-15**. Every entity has a `<slug>.md` (mechanics in our own words, level tables, client comparison) and a `<slug>.json` (normalized levels, constants, client name, mismatches). `index.json` lists all 52 entities.

## Method

1. **Fetch.** Wikitext and revision ids came from the MediaWiki API (`action=parse&prop=wikitext|revid`) using `curl`, one request at a time about 1.1 s apart. Titles were checked with `list=search`. Disambiguation pages (Cannon, Archer Tower, Air Defense, Hidden Tesla, Bomb Tower, X-Bow, Inferno Tower, Super Wizard Tower, Spring Trap, Wall, Defensive Buildings, Traps) were replaced by their `/Home Village` subpages; the `Town Hall/Giga Inferno` tabber was replaced by its TH13-TH16 subpages. Raw JSON stayed in the session scratchpad and is not committed.
2. **Parse.** Scripts extracted Summary, mechanics sections, Statistics/Supercharges tables and the dated History table. They handled `class=...|value` cell prefixes, `{{Res}}`/`{{H}}` templates, row and column spans, two-row headers (such as Inferno ramp stages) and duplicate headers split by resource (Walls: Gold, Elixir and Wall Rings). Units were normalized: times to seconds, ranges to tiles, `N/A` to null. Numeric ranges such as `300-400` or `95s+` also get `...Min`/`...Max` keys.
3. **Client.** Rows in the decoded tables use forward inheritance (each row is merged over the previous one). Units: 1/100 tile for ranges and radii, ms for speeds and delays, minutes for `GearUpTime`, hours for module `BuildTimeH`, and troop `Speed`/12.5 for wiki movement speed. XP is derived as floor(sqrt(build seconds)), which matched every building. Stats kept outside the building row were followed through `Projectile` -> `HitSpell` (spells.csv), `Weapon` (weapons.csv), `MiniLevels` (mini_levels.csv), `SpecialAbilities` (special_abilities.csv), `DefenceTroopCharacter`/guardians.csv -> characters.csv, and the seasonal_defense* tables.
4. **Compare.** Every wiki level row was checked automatically against hitpoints, cost, build time, required Town Hall, XP, DPS and damage per hit (tolerance 0.01 or 0.05%). Each entity also has explicit checks for its special columns (bursts, ramps, chains, bounces, death damage, capacities, module stats, and so on). Per-TH counts come from the wiki `NumberAvailable` template versus the carried-forward townhall_levels.csv columns. The game's own derived table `progression.json` was checked against the wiki as well.
5. **Snapshot dating.** Client 18.400.21 already contains the January 12/28, February 23, April 27, June 16 and **July 14, 2026** balance changes: Logger pushback 1.5 tiles and post-hit range 5/6, X-Bow/Scattershot supercharges, Phase 4 crafted-defense data. It lacks the **August 1, 2026** TH11 Crafting Station access and the **August 31, 2026** Builder's Hut/Monolith supercharges and Hot Candle timer change. The client therefore sits between mid-July and August 2026, and the wiki is slightly newer.

## Most important wiki (live) vs client 18.400.21 findings

1. **Eagle Artillery damage per hit (progression.json bug).** The main shell damage (225/250/275/350/425/475/525, radius 0.75 tiles) lives in spells.csv `Eagle Artillery Hit Spell` via `Projectile` -> `HitSpell` at the building level. The building's `Damage` column (20-50) is only the 3-tile shockwave. `progression.json` uses the shockwave value as damage per hit (and dps 2-5, splash 3, rate 10, no 3-shell burst). The wiki's DPS is 3 x shell / 10 s, i.e. 67.5-157.5. Activation at 200 housing space (`WakeUpSpace`) and `AmmoCount` 30 volleys = 90 shells match.
2. **Town Hall 18 inherits a weapon it should not have.** The TH18 row leaves `Weapon`, `MergeRequirement` and `ActivateAfterSeconds` blank, so forward inheritance gives it the TH17 Inferno Artillery (`Townhall17`) and `Eagle Artillery:7:0`. `progression.json` shows exactly that (dps 40, rate 3.5, range 12, merge). The live TH18 has **no weapon**: `HousesGuardians` is TRUE and one Guardian defends instead. weapons.csv `Townhall18` (a TH18Heal healing weapon, DPS -600) is not referenced by any row.
3. **Town Hall weapon levels and modes.** Since October 6, 2025 the Giga Tesla and Giga Inferno (TH12-16) have no levels; each weapons.csv row is single-level and matches the wiki (4 targets, 10 tiles, 0.5 s / 0.128 s, death bomb, Frost/Poison death spells). Exception: the **TH14 death damage is 900 on the wiki vs `DieDamage` 1,000 in the client**. The Giga weapons wake on damage or at 51% destruction (client global `HIDDEN_BUILDING_APPEAR_DESTRUCTION_PERCENTAGE` 50, presumably 'more than 50'). The TH17 Inferno Artillery has 5 weapon levels (costs and times match), auto-activates at battle start (`ActivateAfterSeconds` 1), and its fire pools come from `TH17WeaponAreaDamage` (75 DPS, 6.8 s, 2.5-tile radius not on the wiki).
4. **Merged-defense rules.** `MergeRequirement` sits on the *result* building as `Name:level:gearedFlag;...`. Ricochet Cannon and Multi-Archer Tower need two non-geared level-21 inputs (flag 0). The Multi-Gear Tower needs the geared-up Cannon and Archer Tower (flag 1). Super Wizard Tower needs two level-17 Wizard Towers. The Inferno Artillery is the TH17 Town Hall row with `Eagle Artillery:7:0`. Merges are permanent and must all be completed before the next Town Hall. townhall_levels.csv never lowers the input counts (Cannon 7, Archer Tower 9, Wizard Tower 6, Eagle Artillery 1 at TH17-18), so remaining inputs must be computed; the wiki shows the Eagle Artillery count dropping to 0 at TH17. Post-January 2026 merged values are in the client (Ricochet bounce 3.5 tiles and -30%, Multi-Archer 0.6 s).
5. **Burst-mode timing ambiguities.** The geared-up Mortar's statistics table (41.6-148.09 per shell) implies a ~5 s cycle, but the same page's February 2026 History quotes in-game values (L9 46, L15 98, L17 120) that match the client's `AltAttackSpeed` 3.5 s + 2 x 0.5 s = 4.5 s cycle. The table is probably stale. Multi-Gear Tower Fast Attack shows 156/165/170 per ball (0.96 s cycle), while the client `AltAttackSpeed` 350 + 3 x 192 = 0.926 s would give 3.5% less. Firespitter per-ball values imply 2.24 s vs the client's 2.216 s. Displayed DPS is often 1 below the client value (Cannon burst, Mortar burst, Firespitter) because the game shows truncated numbers.
6. **Revenge Tower stages.** Stage damage lives in special_abilities `DebrisTowerTier2-4`: stage 1 450/500 at 1.2 s, stages 2-3 225/250 at 0.6 s / 0.35 s with 1 / 2 bounces at -40%, thresholds 5/25/50 destroyed buildings. The supercharge raises every tier by one level (`SpecialAbilityLevelBuff`). The building row's `Damage` 50/60 is not stage damage, but `progression.json` uses it. The client bounce search distance is 4.5 tiles, while the wiki estimates about 2.
7. **Supercharges.** Client mini-level bonuses are cumulative and also apply to gear-up/alt modes (Mortar burst, Multi-Gear Fast Attack). Costs and times match for 14 of 18 buildings. X-Bow charges are 9M/7M on the wiki vs 14M/9M in the client; the wiki numbers equal the Mortar's, so a copy error is likely. The August 31, 2026 Builder's Hut and Monolith charges are missing: only unlinked, older TH17 mini rows exist, with different prices and TH.
8. **Guardians (TH18).** All per-level DPS, HP, upgrade costs/times (upgrade_data `GuardianGeneral`, Elixir 18M-28M, 7-13 days) and ranges match, including the January and July 2026 balance changes. Differences: Longshot search/patrol radius is 17 tiles on the wiki vs `MaxSearchRadiusForDefender` 1600 (16). The client gives Guardians `HousingSpace` 25 while the wiki says they have no housing value. Logger damage radius is undocumented on the wiki (client `PenetratingRadius` 1.8 tiles). Poison at 30% comes from globals `GUARDIAN_POISON_*` = 30. The Smasher's enrage (+60% damage, +12 speed) and death Rage spell (identical to the Spell Tower Rage) match.
9. **Crafting Station and Phase 4 crafted defenses.** The wiki says the station is available from TH11 since August 1, 2026, with module level 1 at TH11. The client row requires TH17, its count exists only at TH18, and module level-1 rows require TH12. Every module stat, cost and time for Hot Candle, Hero Hunter and Cake-A-Pult matches, including all Hot Candle melt thresholds. Differences: the Hot Candle melt timer starts on troop deployment on the wiki (Aug 31) but on the first destroyed building in the client (`ActiveAfterNumBuildingsDestroyed` 1). The Cake-A-Pult bomb delay is 3 s on the wiki vs ~2.3 s spell timing in the client.
10. **Level-1 / Town Hall encodings and small numeric drifts.** Trap level-1 rows use `TownHallLevel` 1, so unlock TH must come from counts. On the Cannon, level 2 is TH1 on its page vs TH2 in the client, and TH1 has 2 Cannons (page) vs 1 (client); the wiki's own Town Hall matrix agrees with the client. Other single-value drifts: Inferno Tower L5 cost 4.2M vs 4.0M; Bomb L4 time 40m vs 30m; Seeking Air Mine L3 time 1d vs 12h; Tornado Trap L1 cost 1.8M vs 1.0M, plus a 3-tile wiki radius vs a 4-tile client spell; Scattershot 3.2 s (wiki) vs `AttackSpeed` 3228; Spell Tower building `AttackRange` 800 vs weapon 900 (the wiki's 9 tiles). The Wall level-19 piece cap (275) is not in the client tables.
11. **Other `progression.json` gaps.** It has no burst/fast-attack modes, no X-Bow air mode, no Inferno ramp or multi-target data, no chain/bounce/pierce data, no Spell Tower spells and no death damage. Air Bomb and Seeking Air Mine are marked as ground traps, Scattershot rate is 3.228 and Spell Tower range is 8. Values that do match the wiki: hp, cost, time and base dps for most single-mode defenses.

## Pages not found or not standalone

- **Merge / Merged Defenses** and **Gear Up**: no such wiki pages exist; searches return only the individual defense pages or user guides. `merge.md` and `gear-up.md` are synthesized from the linked defense pages, and their JSON has `url`/`revid` null plus a `sources` list.
- **Walls**: redirects to the `Wall` disambiguation page; `Wall/Home Village` was used.
- **Town Hall weapon for TH18**: no weapon exists; covered by `Town Hall/Guardians` plus the Longshot, Smasher and Logger pages.
- **Defensive Buildings** and **Traps**: navigation/tabber pages; their Home Village subpages were summarized as mechanics.
- Not covered: former crafted defenses (Hook Tower, Flame Spinner, Crusher Mortar, Hero Bell, Bomb Hive, Light Beam, Roaster, Air Bombs, Lava Launcher), event traps, and Builder Base or Clan Capital variants. The Town Hall page's unlock matrix was not used as a source because it is partly stale (e.g. X-Bow `12 + 2` and Scattershot `6 + 2` at TH18).
