## Mechanics

- The page lists the Home Village defenses in unlock order: Cannon, Archer Tower, Mortar, Air Defense, Wizard Tower, Air Sweeper, Hidden Tesla, Bomb Tower, X-Bow, Inferno Tower, Eagle Artillery, Scattershot, Builder's Hut, Spell Tower, Monolith, Multi-Archer Tower, Ricochet Cannon, Multi-Gear Tower, Firespitter, Revenge Tower, Super Wizard Tower; the Town Hall weapons (Giga Tesla TH12, Giga Inferno TH13-16, Inferno Artillery TH17, Guardians TH18); Walls; Traps.
- It also lists event/crafted defenses outside this dossier: Crafting Station, Hot Candle, Hero Hunter and Cake-A-Pult.
- General rule stated on the page: each defense has strengths and weaknesses, so placement should reflect them.
- Cross-cutting rules gathered from the individual pages: defense-targeting troops (Giants, Balloons, Hog Riders...) only consider buildings flagged as defenses; the Town Hall with an inactive Giga weapon, an upgrading Giga weapon, an upgrading weaponized Builder's Hut and a hidden Hidden Tesla are not treated as defenses until active; the dormant Revenge Tower and empty X-Bow/Inferno/Eagle/Scattershot are still defenses. Defenses under upgrade do not attack.
- Reloadable defenses (X-Bow, Inferno Tower, Eagle Artillery, Scattershot) re-arm for free on login and always appear loaded in Clan War bases.
