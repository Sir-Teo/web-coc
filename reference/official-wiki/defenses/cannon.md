## Mechanics

- Unlocked at Town Hall 1. Count per TH (wiki): TH1 2, TH5 3, TH7 5, TH10 6, TH11 7. From TH16 merged Ricochet Cannons (and at TH17 the Multi-Gear Tower) consume Cannons, so only 3 (TH16) / 0 (TH17+) standalone Cannons remain once every merge is built.
- Footprint 3x3.
- Normal mode: range 9 tiles, one shot every 0.8 s, single target, **ground only**. Damage per shot = DPS x 0.8 (level 1: 7 DPS / 5.6 per shot; level 21: 160 DPS / 128 per shot).
- No minimum range, no splash, no favorite target.
- Gear Up (Burst mode): available from level 7 (TH6) through the Master Builder once a Double Cannon in the Builder Base is level 4 (BH4). Costs 1,000,000 Gold and 2 days. A geared-up Cannon fires bursts of 4 cannonballs, 0.192 s apart, with 1.6 s between bursts; range drops to 7 tiles. Burst DPS is double the normal DPS and each ball hits for 36% more than a normal shot (level 21: 174.08 per ball, shown truncated to 174 in game). The mode is permanent once geared up (no toggle).
- Burst DPS on the wiki is described as an approximation: the full DPS is only reached if all 4 balls of each burst connect.
- Merging: at TH16 two non-geared level 21 Cannons merge into a Ricochet Cannon; at TH17 a geared-up level 21 Cannon plus a geared-up level 21 Archer Tower merge into the Multi-Gear Tower. Because of merging the Cannon receives no new levels and has no Supercharge.

### Recent balance notes (from the page's History table)

- February 23, 2026: geared-up (burst) DPS raised (e.g. level 21 216 -> 320) and burst damage per hit raised by 36% (level 21 128 -> 174.08).
- March 24, 2025: upgrade costs (levels 6-21 and Gear Up 1.5M -> 1M) and times reduced.

**Interpretation of client columns**

- `DPS` is the normal-mode DPS; damage per shot = `DPS` x `AttackSpeed`/1000 (800 ms). `AttackRange` 900 = 9 tiles; `GroundTargets` TRUE / `AirTargets` FALSE.
- Burst mode: `AltAttackMode` TRUE, `AltDPS` (= 2 x `DPS`), `AltAttackSpeed` 1600 ms = time between bursts, `AltBurstCount` 4, `AltBurstDelay` 192 ms, `AltAttackRange` 700. Damage per ball = `AltDPS` x (`AltAttackSpeed` + (`AltBurstCount`-1) x `AltBurstDelay`)/1000/`AltBurstCount` = 320 x 2.176/4 = 174.08 at level 21, matching the wiki at every burst level.
- Gear Up: `GearUpBuilding` = BB Double Cannon, `GearUpLevelRequirement` 3 (0-based, i.e. Double Cannon level 4), `GearUpCost` 1,000,000 and `GearUpTime` 2880 (minutes = 2 days) appear on the level 7 row, the first eligible level; `townhall_levels.csv` has a separate `Cannon_gearup` count column.
- Merge requirements live on the merged buildings (`Ricochet Cannon.MergeRequirement` = `Cannon:21:0;Cannon:21:0`, `Multi Gear Tower.MergeRequirement` = `Archer Tower:21:1;Cannon:21:1`, where the last field is the geared-up flag).
- The game's progression.json exposes only normal-mode dps/rate/range for the Cannon; burst-mode values (AltDPS, burst timing, 7-tile range) are not derived there.
