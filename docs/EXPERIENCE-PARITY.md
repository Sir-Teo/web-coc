# Clash of Clans experience coverage

Updated September 28, 2026. This is an implementation inventory, not a claim of complete live-game parity. The current build is a local village-and-campaign game. A working menu or similar-looking sprite does not count as implementing the corresponding CoC system.

## September 15 asset and progression expansion

The [complete pinned official asset library](../reference/full-client/README.md) is present: 9,075 archived files, 552 building/trap portraits, 271 roster icons, plus runtime timelines for 348 building levels and 305 troop levels. The asset catalog covers all Town Hall levels through 18. The village exposes TH18 upgrade ceilings, 46 building kinds, 32 regular troops, 8 siege machines and 17 super troop variants, full per-type research tables and King levels through 110. High-level gold/elixir storage and collector artwork is source-specific; Dark Spell Factory housing is included. Old TH1–8 economy and combat tables remain compatibility baselines.

Native animations are connected to the current village and battle renderer. The generic combat model handles the expanded troop/defense definitions. Since then the Town Hall 11–18 defenses, their merges, gear-ups, supercharges, Guardians and Town Hall weapons ([NATIVE-DEFENSES.md](NATIVE-DEFENSES.md)) and the complete hero, equipment and pet system ([NATIVE-HEROES.md](NATIVE-HEROES.md)) run from the client tables and are drawn from the client packs. Version 52 adds Workshop siege machines, paid super-troop boosts, local practice defending heroes and baked hero/pet battle art. Donated siege cargo and asynchronous defenses still require a server; registry coverage does not imply exact client behavior. The older sections below document the specialized systems and their historical audits.

## September 28 audit corrections

A three-part audit (village and economy, combat and army, campaign and UX) compared this build with the pinned client and the live game. It corrected rows below that had fallen behind the code, and changed these behaviors:

- A Builder's Hut bought with gems is finished on placement and needs no free builder; the client's hut build time is zero.
- Clearing a tree or rock needs a free builder and holds it until the obstacle is gone.
- The equipment screen says the Star Bonus pays ore. The first coach step now points to Payback. The Settings footer credits Supercell's assets. The fake online dot beside the chief's name is gone. The campaign labels its 47 generated villages as fan-made.

The audit's open findings are recorded in the table below: tutorial, localization, music, and gems and XP. Frozen trophies are answered by [ladder matches](LADDER.md), an invented local stand-in for multiplayer.

## Baseline

The target is the modern Home Village loop. Most artwork is now reconstructed from the pinned client (see Art and animation below). Two verified rules changed the direction of this pass:

- Supercell removed troop, spell, and siege-machine preparation costs in [Home Village Changes, June 2022](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashofclans/blog/news/home-village-changes-2/).
- Supercell announced removal of troop, spell, and siege-machine training time and hero recovery time in [Troop Training: The Wait Is Over!, March 2025](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashofclans/blog/news/troop-training-the-wait-is-over/).

Those sources establish free, instant army preparation. They do not establish that this game's catalog, housing progression, economy, combat numbers, or campaign reproduce the live game. Those remain separately tracked below. Future catalog additions need their own current primary-source checks.

## Defense pass

Skeleton Traps now support two TH8 levels, saved ground/air modes, timed spawning and real defending units. Attackers can retaliate, ground defenders cross their own walls, spells and splash respect allegiance, and portable replays reconstruct both modes. Wizard fireballs use native five-tile/s fixed-point flight. Alert propagation, target scoring and directional art remain incomplete. Campaign Clan Castles now release their defending garrisons, and defending heroes fight in practice and campaign attacks (see Heroes below). See [SKELETON-TRAP.md](SKELETON-TRAP.md).

Hidden Teslas now have six original level sprites, concealed battle targeting and occupancy, six-tile proximity/51% destruction reveal, fast single-target ground/air attacks, Lightning interruption and replay reconstruction. They count toward destruction even while hidden. Upgrading Teslas are visible but inactive. Numerical stats use native client data; emergence timing, art, audio and fractional-threshold semantics remain approximations. See [HIDDEN-TESLA.md](HIDDEN-TESLA.md).

Air Sweepers and Seeking Air Mines now extend the air-defense roster. Sweepers have eight saved directions, four level-specific sprite sets, traveling gusts and zero-damage defense targeting. Mines have distinct armed, flying and spent artwork and distance-based single-target flight. Both appear in later authored campaign stages. Native cone interpretation, push easing and mine target-loss handling remain explicit fidelity gaps; see [AIR-CONTROL.md](AIR-CONTROL.md).

Cannon, Archer Tower, Mortar, Air Defense and Wizard Tower now have explicit reference tables for playable health, cost, duration and normal-mode damage, with compatible handling of accepted older levels. Count limits and TH ceilings are explicit. See [Air Defense / Wizard Tower](AIR-WIZARD-PROGRESSION.md), [Cannon / Archer Tower](DEFENSE-PROGRESSION.md), and [Mortar](MORTAR-PROGRESSION.md). Mortar has six individual level sprites; walls and camps have eight each. Other buildings still share artwork tiers. These audits do not establish parity for troop stats, campaign tuning or the full defense roster.

Bombs, Giant Bombs, Air Bombs, Spring Traps, and Wizard Towers are now playable, with original artwork, shop categories, correct catalog unlock labels, placement, upgrading, and save persistence. Traps are concealed in scouting, campaign miniatures, pointer picking, and the text snapshot until triggered. They never block troop paths or deployment, cannot be attacked or damaged by spells, and do not count toward destruction. Each new practice or campaign attack starts with fresh trap state; upgrading traps remain inactive. Campaign stages 2–12 include authored traps, and stages 6, 8, 10, and 12 include Wizard Towers.

Spring behavior follows Supercell’s [October 2025 update notes](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashofclans/blog/release-notes/get-ready-for-ranked-update/): one largest-housing target, ejection within capacity, a vertical toss and stun for larger survivors, and prevention of simultaneous spring stacking. Heroes take half damage and remain in the village. Explicit modern reference tables now drive all four traps’ damage, costs, timers, capacities and level-dependent blast radii; placement is instant and does not require a free builder. See [TRAP-PROGRESSION.md](TRAP-PROGRESSION.md) for source discrepancies and verification. Spring airtime/height, Giant Bomb fuse, Air Bomb flight and campaign tuning still use local values. Real asynchronous defenses and rearming between those attacks require a server.

The campaign audit still requires a viable opening and a three-star approach for a developed veteran army at every stage. The final fortress defense multiplier was reduced from 1.7339 to 1.55 to keep it reachable after adding splash and traps.

## Next implementation order

1. Extend the explicit building level tables to counts, troop/spell unlocks, housing and economy. Expand troop/spell and defense catalogs together and re-audit the campaign.
2. Finish the hero pass: defending hero abilities, equipment and pets, placement, and the six equipment abilities that still need their own projectile flight.
3. Keep combat-version compatibility explicit as the roster expands; local seeking and portable replay-file sharing now work. Hosted sharing and historical combat engines remain later work.
4. Build accounts, authoritative saves and battle outcomes, then real matchmaking/defense. Clan and war features depend on that foundation.
5. Expand remaining modes and activities while improving per-level art, directional animations, audio, and physical-device quality.

The order favors changes that can be played and verified end to end. It is not a promise that one local build pass can eliminate every gap in a long-running online game.
