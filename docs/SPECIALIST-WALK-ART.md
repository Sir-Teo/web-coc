# Specialist walking artwork

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool on September 11, 2026. These are original sprites, not extracted game assets. The existing portrait files remain intact.

| Troop        | Accepted RGBA source                            | Shipping atlas                                      |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Goblin       | `art/source/specialist-walk-v1/goblin.png`      | `public/assets/characters/walk/goblin-v1.webp`      |
| Wall Breaker | `art/source/specialist-walk-v1/wallbreaker.png` | `public/assets/characters/walk/wallbreaker-v1.webp` |

Each atlas contains four 128 × 128 frames in a 512 × 128 lossless WebP. The source sheets use a 2 × 2 arrangement. `scripts/specialist-walk-assets.mjs` crops the cells before trimming, preserves generated alpha, applies one scale per character, registers the upper-body alpha centroid horizontally, and anchors the feet at y=122. Registering the upper body prevents a changing leg silhouette from pulling the whole character sideways. RGB values in fully transparent pixels do not affect rendered appearance.

Run `node scripts/specialist-walk-assets.mjs` to rebuild, or add `--check` to verify a byte-identical rebuild against the shipped files. `npm run assets` includes the pipeline. Builds do not call an image service. The source PNGs stay outside the shipped public directory.

Earlier Goblin candidates and an edited Wall Breaker sheet were rejected because they painted a checkerboard instead of supplying alpha. An earlier transparent Wall Breaker candidate repeated almost the same leg pose and added goggles. Neither ships. Fresh generations supplied the accepted open and passing poses and real transparency; their native facing direction is lower right.

The runtime uses a 100 ms frame interval for Goblins and 110 ms for Wall Breakers, driven by battle time. The passing frame supplies a planted idle/attack/reduced-motion pose. Their drawn gait replaces additional procedural body bobbing. These intervals are local presentation tuning. This is a mirrored three-quarter animation set; rear views, full directional coverage, and dedicated attack/death artwork remain open.

## Accepted Goblin prompt

Transparent RGBA game sprite atlas, four DIFFERENT sequential running poses in a 2-column 2-row grid. Same small lime-green goblin with huge pointed ears, mischievous grin, red-brown leather vest, brown shorts and belt with gold buckle, bare feet, heavy brown sack filled with gold carried over his back. Same slightly overhead isometric three-quarter view facing lower right, same size and warm painted 3D mobile strategy game style in every cell. Keep head, torso and sack proportions consistent. The FOUR leg poses are: top left—long open running stride, foreground foot reaching diagonally down-right; top right—passing pose with both knees and feet close together underneath the waist; bottom left—opposite running stride, FOREGROUND THIGH points backward toward the LEFT, FOREGROUND KNEE is deeply bent and FOREGROUND FOOT is lifted high behind the goblin, while the farther foot projects forward toward the lower right; bottom right—opposite passing pose with the foreground knee raised forward and farther leg straight under the body. Do not repeat the same leg silhouette in all cells. One grounded foot at a shared baseline inside each equal cell; entire ears, sack, hands and feet contained with generous transparent margins. True transparent background with alpha, including gaps between limbs. No ground plane, shadows, lettering or dividers.

## Accepted Wall Breaker prompt

Production transparent RGBA sprite atlas, 2 columns by 2 rows. Four DISTINCT sequential running poses of the SAME tiny ivory skeleton carrying an oversized round black bomb with a short glowing fuse in both hands, wearing a simple brown leather aviator cap WITHOUT goggles. Same big grinning skull, same upper body, same bomb, same proportions and warm painted 3D mobile strategy-game rendering throughout. All face diagonally lower right from an isometric overhead camera. TOP LEFT: a wide open stride, near leg extending forward and far leg folded behind. TOP RIGHT: closed passing stride, both feet close together directly under the pelvis, near knee slightly bent. BOTTOM LEFT: opposite wide open stride, near knee folded sharply backward with its foot raised behind toward the left, far leg stretching forward toward the right. BOTTOM RIGHT: opposite closed passing stride, both feet close underneath the pelvis, far knee slightly bent. Make the top-right and bottom-right figures' feet visibly close together, clearly unlike the spread legs in the left column. Identical render scale and foot baseline in each equal cell, generous transparent padding. Actual transparent alpha outside the figures, including spaces between bones. No floor, shadows, text or dividers.
