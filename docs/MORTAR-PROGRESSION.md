# Mortar progression and normal-mode combat

The live game now reads all eighteen normal levels from the [pinned client 18.400.21 source tables](../reference/mortar/README.md). That reference contains the complete HP, DPS, gold, time and Town Hall table. The first ten levels keep their previously audited values. Home Town Hall 1–8 counts remain **0, 0, 1, 1, 1, 2, 3, 4**, with level ceilings **0, 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6**. Later levels can be retained in valid villages and replays; the home catalog does not permit their construction or upgrading beyond the Town Hall eight limit.

Normal mode fires every **5 seconds** at ground targets from **4 through 11 tiles**, including both boundaries. Shell damage is five times source DPS and resolves once within an inclusive **1.5-tile ground radius**. Units spawned after the impact instant and flying units are excluded. The selected landing point stays fixed, so troops can escape; destroying the launcher preserves the airborne shot, while ending the raid cancels pending damage.

The original projectile speed is 500 source units: five tiles per second. New attacks use distance divided by this speed for flight duration, ranging from 0.8 to 2.2 seconds across the valid targeting range. Physical timing is separate from the locally projected visual arc. Arrival rounding uses the existing 1e-9-second simulation tolerance; presentation checks the actual resolved impact so a shell cannot remain visible after dealing damage at that boundary.

Combat recording version **35** identifies the timing change. Playback, import and export explicitly support version **34** with its original fixed 1.15-second Mortar flight. That flag is created by the validated replay runner and is not persisted in the home village. Three immutable recordings generated by the actual pre-integration checkout retain all **3,143** prior physical battle states. Earlier incompatible recording versions keep their result summaries but remain unavailable for playback.

`mortar-stats.ts`, the shared defense progression and required-Town-Hall paths supply construction, paid upgrades, Info comparisons and combat. Paid deadlines and health reconciliation retain the existing save behavior. Presentation history holds at most sixteen launches and sixteen resolved impacts per Mortar plus its destruction time, and does not change combat randomness.

The fixed Magic Practice attack now naturally reaches **42% destruction** instead of 33% under the new flight timing. Its **75** shrink pulses, whole-state replay/seek behavior and Node/Chromium/WebKit equality remain checked. High Pressure stays gated by unsupported Cannon level 15; No Flight Zone stays gated by garrison defenders and the Clan Castle. Gear-up mode and Mortar knockback remain unsupported pending native engine corroboration.
