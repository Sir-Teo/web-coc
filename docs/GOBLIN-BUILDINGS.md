# Goblin campaign building foundation

Added September 11, 2026. The battle engine and renderer now support three campaign-only identities: Goblin Town Hall, Goblin Hut and Tutorial Cannon. The twelve authored villages remain the playable campaign; this change prepares the native campaign adapter and does not replace their layouts or progress.

## Native interpretation

The pinned public client bundle is `18.400.21`, fingerprint `7f04bdfdc4124b1f49308423bb8f4aa8b137aae3`. `reference/campaign/npc-buildings.json` is generated from the same verified `logic/buildings.csv` as the campaign catalog. Reproduce it with `python3 scripts/import-native-campaign.py`; `--check` verifies exact generated bytes and the source hashes.

| Native identity | Global ID | Engine archetype | Footprint | Hitpoints | Attack |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Town Hall with NPC export | 1000001 | townhall | 4×4 | 400, 800, 1600, 2000, 2400, 2800, 3300, 3900 at levels 1–8 | Passive |
| Goblin Hut | 1000018 | builder | 2×2 | 250 | Passive |
| Tutorial Cannon | 1000060 | cannon | 3×3 | 250 | Ground only, range 9, 1.6 damage every 0.8 seconds |

Native layout levels are zero-based; `makeNpcBuilding` accepts the engine's one-based levels. Unsupported levels throw instead of being clamped. Town Hall's `ExportNameNpc` is `goblin_townhall_lvl1`. It must use the corresponding Town Hall hitpoints, not the separate 750-HP `Goblin Hall` record (1000017), which appears much later and is not implemented here.

The Goblin Hut has neither a weapon nor troop housing in the building table. Its saved `units` arrays are not defending troops. In particular, the five Balloons in Goblin Forest's Hut template must not become five hostile Balloons. Goblin Castle and special boss defenders remain separate future mechanics.

The tutorial weapon shares the normal level-one Cannon visual exports and its 9-tile range / 0.8-second interval. Its native DPS is 2, compared with the normal level-one Cannon's 10. It uses 1.6-damage projectiles and is not multiplied by the authored campaign's difficulty modifier.

## Persistence and artwork

`Building.npc` preserves identity independently of the existing geometry/targeting archetype. This avoids adding enemy structures to the player's shop and upgrade catalog. Home saves reject all `npc` fields; practice snapshots reject them too. Replay version 26 validates identity/archetype/level combinations and exports the identity explicitly. Earlier recordings keep their result summaries under the existing combat-version policy.

Two generated sprites use real alpha, source-verified material identities and separately measured ground anchors. There is no additional level tint or artificial level enlargement. Exact built-in imagegen prompts, reference links and regeneration instructions are in [the artwork record](../art/source/goblin-buildings-v1/README.md). These are original generated recreations, not native pixel-identical exports. Their foundation projection remains approximate. Existing generic wood/stone destruction sprites are reused; native destruction artwork is still pending.
